🎯 Our Mission

CloudTok is a free, open video sharing platform designed to empower creators and connect communities worldwide. We believe everyone deserves a space to share their stories, express their creativity, and build meaningful connections — without algorithms controlling what you see.

Unlike mainstream platforms, CloudTok puts creators first. No hidden fees, no paywalled features, no data mining. Just pure creative expression shared freely across the globe.

✨ What Makes CloudTok Different

  • 100% Free — Upload, watch, like, comment, and share without ever paying a dime
  • Creator-First — Virtual gifts and tipping go directly to creators, not just the platform
  • Live Streaming — Go live with video/audio calls, screen sharing, and real-time chat
  • Private Messaging — Chat, share GIFs, send gifts, and make video calls with anyone
  • No Algorithm Manipulation — Your feed shows what's popular, not what we want you to see
  • Open Platform — Built with transparent, modern technology you can trust

🛠 Built With

CloudTok is built with modern, reliable technology:

  • Backend: Cloudflare Workers (edge computing for instant response worldwide)
  • Database: Cloudflare D1 (SQLite at the edge)
  • Storage: Supabase (video hosting with CDN)
  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (fast, no framework overhead)
  • Real-Time: WebRTC for peer-to-peer video calls
  • Payments: Paystack (secure payment processing)
