🚀 Getting Started

How do I create an account?

▼

To create a CloudTok account:

  • Go to the Sign Up page
  • Enter your display name, username, email, and password
  • Your password must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
  • Click "Create Account" and you'll be automatically logged in

How do I log in?

▼

Go to the Login page and enter your email and password. If you've forgotten your password, click "Forgot Password" to reset it.

What is CloudTok?

▼

CloudTok is a short-video social media platform where you can discover, watch, upload, and interact with short-form video content from creators around the world.

🎬 Videos

How do I upload a video?

▼

To upload a video:

  • Tap the + button in the bottom navigation bar
  • Select a video file from your device
  • Add a caption describing your video
  • Optionally add tags and select a category
  • Tap "Upload" to share your video

How do I like a video?

▼

Double-tap on any video or tap the heart icon to like it. You can view all your liked videos from your profile page.

How do I save a video?

▼

Tap the bookmark icon on any video to save it. You can access all saved videos from your profile page under "Saved".

How do I leave a comment?

▼

Tap the comment icon on any video to open the comments panel. Type your comment and tap "Send".

How do I start a live stream?

▼

Go to the Live page and tap "Start Live Stream". You'll need to allow camera and microphone access. Your followers will be notified when you go live!

👥 Social

How do I follow someone?

▼

Visit any user's profile and tap the "Follow" button. You'll see their videos in your feed and get notified when they post new content.

How do I send a message?

▼

Visit a user's profile and tap the message icon, or go to the Chat tab in the bottom navigation. You can send text messages to anyone who follows you back.

How do I find people to follow?

▼

Use the Discover tab to browse trending content and find new creators. You can also use the Search feature to find specific users by username.

How do gifts work?

▼

You can send virtual gifts to creators during live streams. First, add funds to your wallet via the Payment page. Then, during a live stream, select a gift to send to the streamer.

⚙️ Account

How do I change my password?

▼

Go to Settings from your profile page. You can update your password from the security section. For security, you'll need to enter your current password.

How do I reset my password?

▼

On the login page, click "Forgot Password". Enter your email address and you'll receive a verification code. Enter the code and create a new password.

How do I update my profile?

▼

Go to your profile page and tap the edit icon. You can update your display name, bio, and avatar photo.

Is my data safe?

▼

Yes! CloudTok uses industry-standard security measures including:

  • PBKDF2 password hashing with salt
  • JWT token authentication
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • CORS protection
  • Input validation and XSS prevention

How do I report abuse?

▼

If you see inappropriate content or behavior, please contact the admin team. We take all reports seriously and will investigate promptly.
