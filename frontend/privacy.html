Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use CloudTok, we collect:

  • Account information: username, email address, display name, avatar, and bio
  • Content: videos, thumbnails, comments, and messages you upload or send
  • Usage data: views, likes, saves, search queries, and interaction patterns
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, IP address, and device identifiers
  • Payment information: transaction records processed through Paystack (we do not store card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Personalize your content feed and recommendations
  • Process transactions and manage your wallet
  • Send notifications about activity on your content
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (D1 database and Workers). We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Paystack: For payment processing only
  • Cloudflare: For hosting and infrastructure
  • Supabase: For video storage
  • Law enforcement: When required by law

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and download your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential notifications

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your personal information within 30 days, though some data may be retained in backups for up to 90 days.

7. Cookies

CloudTok uses local storage and session storage for authentication and preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

CloudTok is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the Service.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us through your CloudTok account or visit our Help Center.
